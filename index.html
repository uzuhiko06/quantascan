QuantaScan

A magnetic X-ray for EV battery packs. Find a bad weld or a shorted cell before the pack leaves the line.

Simulated data. This MVP is a physics-based software model of NV-diamond magnetic imaging. The quantum sensor hardware is not built yet (concept stage, TRL 2).

Magnetic image

Change from a healthy pack. Blue means less field, orange means more. Faint colour means the fault is hidden in sensor noise.

How good must the sensor be?

How the model works: each cell’s connection strip is a straight wire carrying the test current. The field above the pack is computed with the Biot–Savart law at 3 mm standoff, with 1.5% natural cell-to-cell variation. The scan fits one current value per cell to the measured image and flags any cell that deviates by more than the larger of 8% or four times its noise-limited uncertainty. This is a simplified model for a concept-stage demo, not a validated pack simulation.
